How much of the city has turned over?

Pick two years; the charts below shade the window

Nobody tracks individual New Yorkers across decades. But the yearly ledger of births, deaths and moves in section II is enough to estimate how much of the city changed hands between any two years: take the people here at the start, apply each year's exit rate (deaths plus out-moves) to them, and count what remains against everyone born or arrived since.
